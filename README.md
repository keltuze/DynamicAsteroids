# Dynamic Asteroids 2.0.6 FixMagneto (Starsector 0.98a-RC8)

## Overview
Dynamic Asteroids transforms static battlefield backgrounds into a tactical combat hazard with interactive salvage mechanics, derelict rescue operations, magnetic shield gathering, and deep space encounters.

## Key Features in v2.0.6 FixMagneto:
* **Derelict Rescue:** Abandoned hulls drift into combat. Protect them for 60 seconds with HUD tracking to guarantee recovery on the post-battle Salvage Screen.
* **Closed-Loop Shield Magnet:** Active shields act as an advanced tractor beam with safe mooring buffers ($D_{safe} = R_{shield} + R_{target} + 45$), preventing hull penetration and runaway feedback loops.
* **Newtonian Mass Contrast:** Large derelicts remain unyielding gravitational anchors ($a_{target} \propto \text{MassRatio}^2$), while agile player ships smoothly reel in towards the hulk.
* **Deep Space Drift Encounters:** Rare derelicts and caches now drift through open space engagements beyond asteroid belts.
* **Explosive Canisters:** Detonating volatile fuel tanks dealing 1,200 HE damage.
* **Tactical AI & PD Whitelist:** Friendly point defense shatters hazardous rocks while preserving valuable salvage.
* **Hotkeys in Simulator:** `[Ctrl +/-]` adjust speed; `[']` spawn derelict; `[;]` spawn cache.
